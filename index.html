The problem

Tokenization fixed the plumbing. Not the experience.

Private credit, real estate and institutional funds are where serious wealth compounds. Tokenization is making them cheaper to issue, faster to settle and possible to own in smaller sizes.

But an investor today still holds cash in one app, investments across several platforms and the documents that matter in their email. Knowing what you can invest in, what is committed and when you can get your money back takes repeated manual work.

  • AccessOfferings are scattered and hard to compare.
  • TermsFees and exit rules sit in 80-page documents.
  • OwnershipIt's unclear who holds what, and where.
  • PlatformsMost are built for crypto natives or back offices.

Access has scaled. Understanding has not. This is the gap Mintly is built to close.

Mintly's AI reads the approved offering documents and answers in plain language, with a source reference for every term. It explains; it doesn't recommend. Verified records, eligibility rules and your approval govern every transaction, and questions that need specialist judgment go to a person.

When can I get my money out?
Redemptions open once a quarter and need 30 days' written notice. If requests in a quarter exceed 5% of the fund, payouts may be spread over more than one window. Tokenization doesn't change this: your tokens can move instantly, but the fund's redemption terms still apply.
